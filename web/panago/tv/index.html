Before it goes on the TV

  • Franchise agreement. Kiran runs the Ontario stores and can promote locally; confirm the agreement covers a branded contest and a store-held customer list.
  • Delivery promise. Pages 4 and 6 say Platterly orders have no delivery fee and no surcharges, at walk-in prices. Platterly orders reach the store in the portal (Orders tab); confirm who delivers before launch.
  • Lawyer read of the contest rules (no purchase necessary, published odds, skill-testing question). About 1 to 2 hours.
  • Deal of the day. The deals on page 5 are the ones panago.com lists for 133 Bremner Blvd. Confirm they also run at Gerrard St W.
  • Package prices. The owner sets the final catering price. The totals shown are menu prices with no discount.
  • Lead capture. Sign-ups, coupon codes and catering requests save to the store database while the store laptop is on. If it is off, the phone holds the sign-up and sends it later.

To run on the TV: open this page in the TV's browser with ?s=g#tv (Gerrard) or ?s=b#tv (Bremner) at the end of the link. It plays all six pages full screen, 10 seconds each, and each code tells us which store and page was scanned.
